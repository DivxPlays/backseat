# Backseat: its look

Recorded on 2026-11-10 by the starter kit, so new projects' looks don't repeat it.

- **Kind:** an app (a tray app with an on-screen character overlay). It has no launcher; the tray menu's "About
  Backseat..." shows the name and version.
- **Identity:** the original characters Warlord Vorn and Mika, and their icons (`docs/images/icon-vorn.png`,
  `icon-mika.png`).
- **Trailer:** its own script, `make_trailer.py`: a comedy reel led by the characters over the user's own clips
  (Medal recordings). It isn't one of Repo Trailers' style presets.
- **Taken for other projects:** a character-led comedy reel as a trailer.

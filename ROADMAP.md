# Roadmap

Planned, not built yet. Order: full body, then Bloodfire (it draws on top of the body), then the free local model, then Discord.

## Full body
- His whole body instead of the bust, scaled to fit about the space he uses now.
- More animations: idle stance, arms crossed, pointing at you, laughing, slumping when you lose.

## Bloodfire (power-up form)
- His own original form: his black hair ignites into dark-red flame, his eyes burn white, his armor cracks with molten light.
- Triggers: big plays (epic plays and win streaks; he powers up for a few seconds, then cools down) and reaching 100 respect.

## Free local model (mix)
- A free vision model on your RTX 4090 (through Ollama) makes his automatic comments; Claude still answers when you talk to him. Roughly halves the bill.
- Needs Ollama and a model download (several GB), which you approve first. It shares the GPU with your game and could cost a few frames.

## Discord calls (mic option)
- Needs VoiceMeeter (free) installed first.
- His voice mixed into your mic through VoiceMeeter, so everyone in the call hears him.
- A second ear on Discord's sound, so friends can say "Hey Vorn" too.
- He knows who's talking: your mic is you, Discord is a friend, and voice fingerprints tell friends apart (fallback: they say their name).
- Foul-mouthed squad roasts: his own grudge list and running jokes for each friend, and he pits you against each other. No slurs.
- Friends' questions come out of your monthly budget; consider sending them to Haiku.

## Done
- Cheaper calls: web search only when asked (questions 4.6 -> 1.9 cents), smaller screenshots of displays without the game (comments 0.07 -> 0.05 cents). Prompt caching doesn't apply: the repeated part of a call is below the minimum length that can be cached.

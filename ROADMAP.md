# Roadmap

Planned, not built yet.

## Discord calls (mic option)
- His voice mixed into your mic through VoiceMeeter, so everyone in the call hears him.
- A second ear on Discord's sound, so friends can say "Hey Vorn" too.
- He knows who's talking: your mic is you, Discord is a friend, and voice fingerprints tell friends apart (fallback: they say their name).
- Foul-mouthed squad roasts: his own grudge list and running jokes for each friend, and he pits you against each other. No slurs.
- Friends' questions come out of your monthly budget; consider sending them to Haiku.

## Installer
One setup file that installs him on another PC: Python, everything he needs, a Start menu shortcut, and a prompt for the API key.

## Move his memory between PCs
Export and import his memory (stats, respect, grudges, lore, notes, optionally the Hall of Shame) from the tray menu.

## Full body
A full-body character instead of the bust, with more animations: idle stance, arms crossed, pointing at you, laughing, slumping when you lose.

## Black hair and black eyes
Change his hair and eyes to black.

## Power-up form
A transformation he triggers on epic plays or win streaks, with his own original name and look.
